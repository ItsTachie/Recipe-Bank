
# 📖 Overview

> A regional American cuisine that originated in Texas from Tejano culinary traditions blended with Mexican, Spanish, and Anglo-American influences

---

### 💡 General Tips & Ratios

- DIY Taco Seasoning Ratio: Skip the store-bought packets. Mix 2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tbsp ground cumin, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp dried oregano, and 1 tsp salt (add cayenne or red pepper flakes for heat).
- Bloom Your Spices: Add your dry spices to the pan with the meat or aromatics and toast them in the oil for 30–60 seconds before adding any liquid. This wakes up the essential oils and gives the dish a deeper, smokier flavor.
- Warm Your Tortillas: Never serve tortillas straight out of the bag. Toast them in a dry, hot skillet for 20–30 seconds per side until pliable and lightly charred, then wrap them in a clean kitchen towel to steam.
- Balance the Richness: Tex-Mex leans heavily on beef, beans, and cheese. Always finish your plate with fresh acid—like a squeeze of lime juice, pickled jalapeños, or fresh pico de gallo—to cut through the fat.
