# Chili con carne

> *A rich, deeply savory Tex-Mex staple that gets even better the next day. Pairs perfectly with steamed white rice, crispy roasted potatoes, jacket potatoes, or tortilla chips for scooping.*

**Prep time:** `15 mins` | **Cook time:** `60 mins` | **Servings:** `3`

---
## 🛒 Ingredients

**Main**

- Beef mince | 500g 
- Onion
- Garlic
- Bell peppers
- Tomato paste 
- Chopped tomatoes | 1 can
- Red kidney beans | 1 can
- Beef stock
- Cilantro
- Cheddar cheese

**Sauce / Spices**

- Ground cumin
- Smoked paprika
- Dried oregano
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Dark chocolate | 1 square

---
## 🍳 Instructions

1. **Prep:** Dice the onion and bell pepper, mince the garlic, and drain and rinse the kidney beans. Chop up the cilantro
2. **Brown the mince:** Turn the heat up to medium-high, add the beef mince, and let it sit undisturbed for a few minutes at a time so it develops a deep brown crust before breaking it up. Once done set it aside.
3. **Cook:** Fry the onion and pepper for 5–6 minutes until softened, then add the garlic for 1 minute. Then add the mince back in.
4. **Combine:** Stir in the tomato paste and all the spices, cooking for 2 minutes until dark and fragrant. Pour in the chopped tomatoes, beef stock, and kidney beans, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer, partially covered, for ~60 minutes stirring every 15 minutes. Stir in the dark chocolate (if using) during the last 5 minutes and cilantro.
5. **Serve:** Spoon into bowls over rice or potatoes, topped with grated cheddar, a dollop of sour cream or Greek yoghurt, and fresh coriander or spring onions.
