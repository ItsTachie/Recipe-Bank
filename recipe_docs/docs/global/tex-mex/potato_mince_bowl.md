# Potato mince bowl

> *A loaded, high-texture comfort bowl that hits every note: glass-crispy bicarb-roasted potatoes, deeply savoury spiced beef mince, bright crunchy pico de gallo, and a cool, tangy lime-parsley white sauce.*

**Prep time:** `20 mins` | **Cook time:** `45 mins` | **Servings:** `3`

---
## 🛒 Ingredients

**Main**

-  Potatoes 
- Onion | 1 medium
- Red onion | 1 medium
- Garlic | 3 cloves
- Tomato on the vine | 3 medium sized
- Beef mince | 500g
- Tomato paste
- Bicarbonate of soda 
- Cilantro
- Cheddar cheese 
- White Sauce
-  Parsley
- Lime 
- Yoghurt
- Mayo

**Sauce / Spices**

- Paprika
- Cumin
- Salt 
- Black Pepper 
- Oregano

## 🍳 Instructions

1. **Prep:** Cube the potatoes sit them in some cold water, chop up the mince onions and garlic. Make some [pico](../../staples/rice/modules/pico.md) (save the tomato guts) and [sauce](../../staples/rice/modules/garlic_parsley_sauce.md)
2. **Parboil & Roast Potatoes:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and stir in the bicarbonate of soda. Add the potatoes and parboil for 7–8 minutes until the outsides are fork-tender. Drain thoroughly, return them to the dry pot for 1 minute to steam off excess moisture, then shake vigorously to rough up the edges. Toss with 2 tbsp oil, salt, and pepper, spread on a baking tray with space between them, and roast for 35–40 minutes (flipping halfway) until deeply golden and crisp.
3. **Cook the Mince:** While the potatoes roast, heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Fry the diced onion for 4–5 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 1 minute. Crank the heat to medium-high, add the mince, and break it up—let it sit undisturbed for a couple of minutes at a time so it properly browns rather than steams.
4. **Combine & Simmer:** Stir in the tomato paste and all the mince seasonings, cooking for 2 minutes until dark and fragrant. Pour in the splash of water or stock and the tomato guts, scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan, and simmer on low for 3–5 minutes until rich and glossy.
5. **Serve:** Pile the crispy potatoes into bowls, spoon the hot mince over the top, add a generous scoop of cold pico de galo, grate some cheese on top, and drizzle with the white sauce.
